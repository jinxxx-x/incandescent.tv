Incandescent Studios

We craft cinematic narratives that leave a lasting mark. Founded by veteran producers, driven by a vision for what's next.

Incandescent Studios is an independent film and television production company dedicated to creating bold, cinematic storytelling for audiences around the world.

Founded by a team of Emmy-winning producers with decades of collective experience, Incandescent Studios combines creative vision, production expertise, and a proven track record of delivering premium entertainment. Our founders' body of work includes acclaimed series such as The Patriot War with Kelsey Grammer (Fox Nation), Wyatt Earp and the Cowboy War (Netflix), Sitting Bull (History), American Playboy: The Hugh Hefner Story (Amazon), Roman Empire (Netflix), Liberty Or Death: Boston Tea Party with Rob Lowe (Fox Nation) and The Men Who Built America (History). From development through delivery, we partner with leading networks, streaming platforms, studios, and brands to bring compelling documentary, scripted, and unscripted entertainment to life.

Lorenzo De Boni

Founder

With over 15 years of experience, Lorenzo has built a career spanning scripted and unscripted television. Prior to founding Incandescent Studios, he worked on HBO’s Girls and at Stephen David Entertainment, where his credits included Netflix’s Wyatt Earp & The Cowboy War and Fox Nation’s The Patriot War with Kelsey Grammer. Outside of the office, he is an avid Arsenal F.C. and New York Knicks fan.

Tim Kelly

Founder

Tim is a Primetime Emmy Award-winning producer, editor, writer and showrunner with over 20 years of industry experience. Starting his storytelling journey through editing, he moved from the edit room to the writers’ room and eventually to set. After starting in reality TV and news and then moving into premium documentaries and scripted series, he is thrilled to continue telling stories of emotion, character and history.

Jonathan Soule

Founder

After spending over a decade as one of New York’s most sought-after editors, with credits ranging from Queer Eye and Chopped to NOVA, Jonathan expanded into producing premium documentaries and scripted series, earning a Primetime Emmy Award along the way. His projects include The Men Who Built America, Wyatt Earp & The Cowboy War and Human: The World Within, which he conceived for Netflix and PBS.
