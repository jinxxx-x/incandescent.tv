Identity Review · 2026

One identity.
Three expressions.

A focused comparison of the original Incandescent wordmark, the current modern mark, and one considered evolution. The palette, typography, and applications stay constant so the logo—not an unrelated brand world—is what changes.

The constants

What cannot change.

Incandescent should feel like the work it makes: cinematic, intelligent, warm, and precise. The identity should frame the stories rather than compete with them.

01

Cinematic

Built for motion, title cards, decks, and dark viewing environments.

02

Editorial

Serif and sans-serif contrast creates a deliberate high–low rhythm.

03

Warm, not rustic

Ember orange brings light without drifting into nostalgia or western cliché.

04

Work first

The logo needs to sit quietly beside strong imagery and recognizable credits.

Choose the mark.
Then finish the system.

The current wordmark is the strongest baseline. Splitframe is the more expressive challenger: distinct enough to create a recognizable title-card system, but still grounded in the current type and color language. The original remains a useful editorial reference, but it is less reliable at small sizes.
