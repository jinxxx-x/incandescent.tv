Decision 02

Decide what the bulb is allowed to do.

The outline bulb already works as a transparent favicon. The remaining question is whether it stays a quiet utility mark or becomes a visible part of the broader identity.

We need: utility mark only, or supporting brand symbol
Incandescent Studios
Option A

Utility mark only

The bulb appears in the browser tab, social avatar, and other spaces where the full wordmark cannot fit. The wordmark remains the only public-facing logo.

Benefit: quieter and more premium. Risk: the bulb never develops recognition beyond a functional icon.

16 32 64
Option B

Supporting symbol

The bulb may also appear on deck covers, end cards, profile images, and branded transitions. It becomes a recognizable shorthand for Incandescent.

Benefit: builds an ownable visual asset. Risk: needs disciplined use so it does not feel literal or cute.

Recommendation

Current wordmark.
Utility bulb.

This combination is the clearest and most professional starting point. It respects the supplied logo, performs reliably in light and dark environments, and keeps the bulb useful without asking it to carry the whole brand.
